Twinform 生成的是报价单或形式发票（quotation / pro forma invoice）。
它不是中国增值税发票，也不是任何税务机关出具或认可的发票。
本工具不计算应纳税额，不构成税务或法律建议。
单据上的税费行由使用者自行填写，仅用于展示。
所有单据数据只保存在使用者的浏览器中，不会上传到服务器。

Twinform produces quotations or pro forma invoices.
It is not a China VAT invoice and is not issued or endorsed by any tax authority.
It does not calculate tax liability and does not constitute tax or legal advice.
Any tax line is entered by the user and shown for document purposes only.
Document data stays in the user's browser and is not uploaded to a server.
